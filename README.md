# OpsPilot AI
### AI-Assisted Enterprise Business Operations & Automation Platform

OpsPilot AI is an AI-assisted enterprise platform designed to simplify and improve business operations through intelligent workflows, workforce coordination, work order management, and AI-assisted decision-making.

Our goal is to build a scalable, secure, and modern enterprise application using Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, Angular, React, Microservices, and AI technologies.

## Project Vision

To develop a unified business operations platform that helps organizations manage their workforce, tasks, work orders, approvals, and operational activities through intelligent automation.

## Target Industry

Initially, OpsPilot AI focuses on IT services and field-operations organizations.

The platform aims to address practical business challenges through workflow automation, operational visibility, and AI-assisted productivity.

## Key Features (Planned)

- Organization & Employee Management
- Task Management & Assignment
- Work Order Management
- Workflow & Approval Management
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- AI Operations Assistant
- AI-Based Task Classification
- RAG-Based Knowledge Assistant
- AI-Powered Report Summarization
- Analytics & Reporting
- Real-Time Notifications

*Note: These features represent the proposed product scope. Final MVP features will be confirmed in the Product Requirements Document (PRD).*

## Architecture

The platform is planned around modular enterprise architecture, domain-based business capabilities, REST API integration, and secure communication.

The initial architecture will be evaluated between a modular monolith and microservices approach before final approval.

Detailed architecture documentation will be maintained as the project progresses.
