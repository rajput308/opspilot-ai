
# OpsPilot AI
### Intelligent Business Operations & Automation Platform

OpsPilot AI is an AI-powered enterprise platform designed to simplify and automate business operations through intelligent workflows, workforce management, and AI-assisted decision-making.

Our goal is to build a scalable, secure, and modern enterprise application using Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, Angular, React, and AI technologies.

## Project Vision

To develop a unified business operations platform that helps organizations manage their workforce, tasks, work orders, approvals, and operational activities through intelligent automation.

## Key Features (Planned)

- Organization & Employee Management
- Task Management & Assignment
- Work Order Management
- Workflow & Approval Management
- Role-Based Access Control
- AI Operations Assistant
- AI-Based Task Classification
- RAG-Based Knowledge Assistant
- AI-Powered Report Summarization
- Analytics & Reporting
- Real-Time Notifications
